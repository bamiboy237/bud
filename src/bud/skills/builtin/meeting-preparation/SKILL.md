# Prepare a meeting

Identify the meeting and intended outcome from the request and calendar. Use known
context before asking for attendees, dates or background the person already supplied.
If several meetings match and the choice changes the work, clarify which one.

Read relevant earlier discussions, notes and documents. Separate confirmed decisions,
open questions, completed actions and unresolved work. Look for changes since the last
meeting. Keep source references and mark missing context.

Prepare what helps this meeting: the objective, a concise agenda, decisions needed,
questions to raise, relevant facts and preparation work. Adjust detail and structure to
the person's preferences and meeting type. Avoid generic agendas and invented owners.

Check names, dates and links against the sources. Deliver the requested agenda,
pre-read or conversational summary. Publishing, sharing and calendar changes use their
own tools and approvals; do not assume preparation authorizes those actions.
