# Research

Establish the question, the decision it supports, and the requested depth from the
conversation. Ask only for missing information that would materially change the work.

Search relevant sources, then read the strongest originals. Prefer current primary
documentation for capabilities and policies. Community reports are useful observations;
check their dates, setup, follow-ups and counterexamples. Follow promising questions
across sources instead of stopping at search snippets.

Keep each important finding tied to its source. Distinguish documented facts, observed
results, estimates and unresolved claims. Resolve contradictions where possible.
Explain trade-offs and recommend a direction when the evidence supports one.

Match the person's requested detail and format. Include useful source links and clear
answers to the original questions. Save a document when requested, using the available
workspace or document tools. On a follow-up, use retained sources and investigate what
changed rather than inventing a new account of the research.
